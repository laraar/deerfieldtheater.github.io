Volunteer
Deerfield Theater is entirely community-powered, and every show depends on volunteers working behind the scenes as much as the cast on stage. No experience is necessary — just a willingness to pitch in. We'd love to have you join us!
There are lots of ways to get involved, including:
Set Construction
Set Decoration & Painting
Costumes & Wardrobe
Props
Hair & Makeup
Backstage Crew / Stagehands
Lighting & Sound
House Management & Ushering
Concessions
Publicity & Marketing
Program & Playbill Design
Fundraising & Sponsorships
Ready to get involved? Let us know how you'd like to help by filling out our volunteer interest form:
